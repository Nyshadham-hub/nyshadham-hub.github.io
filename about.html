About

My work starts from one question: how far can a satellite-derived map be trusted? As an M.Tech student in Geoinformatics at the National Institute of Technology, Warangal, I answer it against ground truth, testing elevation models on 3,014 Survey of India benchmarks across Telangana.

As a project intern at IIST Thiruvananthapuram, I work on determining ground reality: establishing what is actually on the surface and measuring how closely satellite products agree with it. Alongside, I am building a machine-learning framework that maps river pollutants from PRISMA and EnMAP hyperspectral imagery and identifies the stream most affected within a 50 km buffer of the river.

I came to geoinformatics through civil engineering and a year as a Trainee Engineer at Indian Oil Corporation, managing pipeline works, safety inspections and refuelling logistics. Working to specifications, budgets and deadlines left me with a habit I still keep: a result is finished only when it is validated, documented and usable by someone else.

I am seeking Geospatial Analyst and GIS Analyst roles in remote sensing, Earth observation analytics and GeoAI.
