DEM Accuracy Assessment for Telangana

PythonGDALPyProjQGIS
  • Validated SRTM, NASADEM, CartoDEM, ALOS AW3D30 and Copernicus GLO-30 against 3,014 Survey of India levelling benchmarks.
  • Copernicus ranked most accurate (ubRMSE 5.13 m), then ALOS (5.26 m), NASADEM (5.40 m), SRTM (5.76 m), CartoDEM (6.34 m).
  • Converted EGM2008 to EGM96 with the NGA grid, removing a geoid bias of up to 1.5 m.
  • Accepted at IEEE InGRASS 2026.

Sentinel-1 InSAR DEM Pipeline

PythonSNAPSNAPHUGDAL
  • End-to-end pipeline: queries ASF for Sentinel-1 IW SLC scenes over Telangana and selects pairs by temporal and perpendicular baseline.
  • Automates the 14-step SNAP workflow through GPT graphs, from precise orbits to terrain correction.
  • Mosaics sub-swath DEMs into a 30 m GeoTIFF with resume, logging and fallback geocoding.
  • Ongoing: validation against the same benchmarks and extension to NISAR.
